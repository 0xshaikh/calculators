Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. LotCal is a static website — it collects as little as it possibly can.

1. What data we collect

LotCal is a static site with no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or any personal details to use the calculator. The only things that may be collected are:

  • Essential cookies / local storage — used to remember your cookie choice and whether you’ve seen the disclaimer. These are necessary for the site to work and cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies — only if you accept them. These help us understand which pages are visited and how the calculator is used, so we can improve it. If you reject them, none are set.
  • Advertising cookies — only if you accept them. Third-party ad networks may set them to show you relevant ads (see below).
  • IP address and technical data — your IP and browser details are seen by the hosting provider and any third-party analytics or ad network you consent to, in the ordinary course of serving a web page. We do not store or look at them ourselves.

2. Google AdSense and third-party ads

LotCal may display advertising served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits to LotCal and elsewhere. Third-party vendors and ad networks may also set their own cookies to show you personalised ads.

3. Cookies for personalised ads and how to opt out

Google may use the “cookies for personalised ads” mechanism. You can opt out of personalised advertising by:

  • Visiting Google’s Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com, and
  • Visiting www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising.

You can also block cookies entirely in your browser, or use the cookie banner on this site to accept only essential cookies. Opting out of personalised ads does not stop ads from appearing — it just makes them less targeted.

4. Your choices

Use the cookie banner on any page to accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time, or clear your cookies in your browser settings. Essential local storage items (lotcal_cookie, lotcal_disc) are used only for the site to remember your preferences.

5. Data deletion

Because we store no personal data on a server, there is usually nothing for us to delete. If you want us to remove anything you’ve sent us by email, or to confirm what we hold, email Cryptowithus.help@gmail.com with the subject “LotCal data request”. We’ll confirm within 30 days.

6. GDPR — visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR apply to any personal data we process:

  • Legal basis: essential cookies are processed on the basis of legitimate interest (making the site work); analytics and advertising cookies are processed only on the basis of your consent, which you give or withhold through the cookie banner.
  • Your rights: you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies or rejecting non-essential cookies in the banner.
  • Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority if you believe your data is handled unlawfully.
  • Contact: for any GDPR request, email Cryptowithus.help@gmail.com with the subject “GDPR request”.

Data is not transferred outside these regions knowingly; if it is (for example, via a third-party ad server), it is on the basis of the consent you give or standard contractual safeguards.

7. Children

LotCal is a trading-education tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.
