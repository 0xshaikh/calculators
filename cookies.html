Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This explains the cookies and local storage LotCal uses, and how you control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. LotCal also uses browser local storage for the same purpose. They help the site remember your preferences and let third parties, with your consent, understand traffic and show ads.

2. Essential cookies / local storage

NamePurposeCan you refuse?
lotcal_cookieRemembers your cookie consent choiceNo — needed for the banner to work
lotcal_discRemembers you’ve dismissed the disclaimer noticeNo — needed for the notice

These do not track you. They only store a yes/no preference on your own device.

3. Analytics cookies

If you accept them, we or a third-party analytics provider may set cookies to count visits and see which pages are popular. This helps us improve the site. They are set only with your consent via the cookie banner.

4. Advertising cookies

If you accept them, advertising networks such as Google AdSense may set cookies to show ads relevant to you, including Google’s “cookies for personalised ads” mechanism. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

5. Your consent

The cookie banner on every page asks you to Accept all or accept Only essential. If you choose “Only essential”, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. You can change your mind at any time by clearing the site’s cookies in your browser settings and reloading — the banner will ask again. Your choice is stored in lotcal_cookie.

6. EU / UK visitors

For visitors in the European Union, the UK and Switzerland, consent-based cookies are set only after you actively accept them in the banner, in line with the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. Essential items are required for the site to function.

7. Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may reset your preferences and show the banner again.
