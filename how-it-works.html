Methodology

How It Works

The exact formula, the assumptions behind it, and what LotCal deliberately does not do.

The formula

quantity = lot size × contract size
position value = quantity × price (for USD-quote pairs)
position value = quantity (for USD-base pairs — price not used)
required leverage = position value ÷ account balance
P&L per $1 move = lot size × contract size (USD-quote pairs)

For USD-base pairs (USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD) the position value in USD is lot × contract size, so the price field is disabled and no P&L per $1 is shown (a $1 move in JPY isn’t a $1 move in USD).

Assumptions

  • Contract sizes are common broker defaults: 1 lot gold/platinum/palladium = 100 oz, silver = 5,000 oz, forex = 100,000 units, crypto = 1 coin.
  • Margin: your entire balance is assumed available as initial margin. Real brokers reserve more (margin calls, maintenance margin), so the real requirement is higher.
  • Prices are reference snapshots (4–6 Nov 2026) and are not live — replace them with your broker’s quote.
  • No fees — spread, commission, swap and funding are ignored.

Limits

  • It does not know your broker’s margin tiers, leverage cap, spread or liquidation rules.
  • It does not predict price, place trades or hold funds.
  • Results are estimates for education, not a guarantee a broker will accept the order.
  • It is not financial advice.
