Quick rules of thumb

  • Gold (XAUUSD): 1 lot = 100 oz. 0.01 lot = 1 oz, so P&L is $1 per $1 move.
  • Silver (XAGUSD): 1 lot = 5,000 oz. 0.01 lot = 50 oz, so P&L is $50 per $1 move.
  • Forex majors: 1 lot = 100,000 units of the base currency. 0.01 lot = 1,000 units.
  • Crypto: 1 lot = 1 coin. 0.01 lot = 0.01 BTC (or 0.01 ETH, 0.01 SOL, etc.).

Use the calculator with your balance to turn any of these into a leverage figure.
